# CondoWizard Blog Content Plan

Internal working document. Snapshot: 2026-07-25. Governed by `docs/content-standard.md`.

## Guiding principles

- Ship one authoritative buyer-education cluster before any secondary cluster. Depth first, breadth later.
- Every article resolves a real Toronto purchase decision, not a top-of-funnel definition.
- Every article can be internally linked from at least one project, neighbourhood, or developer page.
- Every article carries the same author identity: Tal Shelef, Sales Representative, Rare Real Estate Inc., Brokerage.
- Every article includes at least one differentiator required by section 13 of the standard: original cost example, decision table, timeline, checklist, contract-clause explanation, end-user vs investor comparison, Toronto-specific scenario, or a Tal note prompt.

## Editorial guardrails

- No sensational titles or invented client stories.
- No unsupported statistics.
- Every regulatory, tax, warranty, or rebate figure cited to the current primary source.
- 2 to 4 `[TAL'S PRACTICAL NOTE: ...]` placeholders in every article, clearly labelled for the human review pass.
- Every article carries a visible `_Last reviewed_` line and a `Sources` list.
