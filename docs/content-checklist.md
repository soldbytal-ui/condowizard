# CondoWizard Content Checklist

Condensed mandatory checklist. Applies to every blog article before it is committed to the repository or published to Supabase. Full guidance lives in `content-standard.md`.

Author: Tal Shelef, Sales Representative, Rare Real Estate Inc., Brokerage.

## 1. Research

- [ ] Primary sources identified (government, regulator, municipal, developer disclosure, official transit).
- [ ] Every legal, tax, fee, rate or deposit figure verified against a current source.
- [ ] Any figure that cannot be confirmed is flagged in the article as needing verification.
- [ ] Source list captured with title, organization, URL and access date.

## 2. Search intent

- [ ] Primary intent classified (educational, comparison, transactional, legal, financial planning, urgent problem, local discovery).
- [ ] Central question answered within the first 150 words.
- [ ] Opening does not use banned openers (world-class city, exciting journey, etc.).

## 3. Keyword plan

- [ ] One clear primary keyword.
- [ ] 6 to 12 secondary keywords.
- [ ] 6+ question keywords.
- [ ] Related entities listed.
- [ ] Confirmed no existing CondoWizard page targets the same primary keyword; overlap resolved by expanding, not duplicating.

## 4. Structure

- [ ] SEO title 50 to 60 characters, unique, non-sensational.
- [ ] Meta description 145 to 160 characters.
- [ ] URL slug lowercase, concise, no unnecessary dates.
- [ ] One H1 aligned with the title.
- [ ] Opening answer (100 to 150 words).
- [ ] 3 to 6 key takeaways with actual information.
- [ ] Table of contents if the article exceeds 1,500 words.
- [ ] Logical H2/H3 hierarchy, one buyer question per section.
- [ ] At least one worked Toronto or Ontario example with labelled assumptions.
- [ ] Risks and misunderstandings section.
- [ ] Practical checklist for the reader.
- [ ] 5 to 10 non-repetitive FAQs.
- [ ] Non-repetitive conclusion.
- [ ] Contextual call to action without fake urgency.

## 5. Professional boundaries

- [ ] No definitive legal, tax, mortgage or accounting conclusions.
- [ ] No guarantees of appreciation, rental income, mortgage approval, completion dates, HST rebate eligibility, or assignment approval.
- [ ] Contract-dependent items flagged as such.

## 6. Style

- [ ] Canadian spelling throughout.
- [ ] No em dashes.
- [ ] No banned phrases (navigate the complexities, delve into, in today's ever-changing market, unlock the potential, game changer, seamlessly, robust, comprehensive landscape, vibrant tapestry, in conclusion, location location location, etc.).
- [ ] No invented quotations, statistics, or client stories.
- [ ] Short and medium-length paragraphs, natural transitions.

## 7. Expert input

- [ ] 2 to 4 `[TAL'S PRACTICAL NOTE: ...]` prompts where authentic experience would add value.
- [ ] Nothing is fabricated as Tal's experience. Placeholders are clearly labelled for later human review.

## 8. Examples and calculations

- [ ] Every number has stated inputs (price, deposit, rate, date checked).
- [ ] Exclusions labelled (land transfer tax, legal fees, adjustments not modelled, etc.).
- [ ] No false precision. No copied calculations from competing sites.

## 9. Internal linking

- [ ] Link to the main Toronto pre-construction hub.
- [ ] At least one neighbourhood page linked when relevant.
- [ ] Relevant developer or project pages linked when relevant.
- [ ] At least one related buyer guide linked.
- [ ] A contact or registration page linked.
- [ ] Descriptive anchor text (no click here / read more / this page).

## 10. External citations

- [ ] Regulatory, tax, deposit, warranty, prescribed-rate, and government-announcement claims cite official sources.
- [ ] `Sources and last reviewed` section at the bottom with an ISO or long-form date.

## 11. Images

- [ ] 3 to 6 image recommendations with filename, alt text, caption and placement.
- [ ] Original diagrams, timelines or comparison tables prioritized over decorative stock.
- [ ] Alt text is descriptive, not keyword-stuffed.

## 12. Schema and technical SEO

- [ ] Structured data specified (Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, Person, Organization).
- [ ] FAQPage schema only when Q&A appears visibly on the page.
- [ ] Author, brokerage, publication date, last-updated date, reading time, canonical URL, OG title/description/image included in metadata.
- [ ] No fake reviews or ratings.

## 13. Differentiation

- [ ] At least one of: original cost example, original decision table, original process timeline, original checklist, contract-clause explanation, end-user vs investor comparison, Toronto-specific scenario, practical note from Tal.
- [ ] No paragraph generic enough to appear on another realtor's site.

## 14. Final review

- [ ] Question answered immediately.
- [ ] Facts current and cited.
- [ ] Financial math independently correct.
- [ ] No improper legal or tax advice.
- [ ] Internal links present and descriptive.
- [ ] CTA relates to the topic.
- [ ] Reviewed/updated date present.
- [ ] Tal's practical notes completed or removed intentionally.
- [ ] Meaningfully different from existing CondoWizard content.
